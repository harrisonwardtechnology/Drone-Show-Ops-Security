# Security

This is an operations security guide for shows that fly over people. No code to exploit, and the reporting stakes here are the highest on this account.

**Guidance that could put a crowd at risk.** If anything here would lead an operator toward an unsafe practice, a wrong failsafe assumption, or misplaced confidence, open an issue immediately, it outranks everything else in every queue.

**Anything attack-enabling or show-identifiable.** Nothing here should help someone interfere with a real show or identify a specific operation's weaknesses. If you spot either, do not open a public issue. Use GitHub's private vulnerability report on this repo, or the contact on [my profile](https://github.com/HarrisonWard), and it gets handled quietly and quickly.

No bounty program. The reward is a crowd somewhere looking up at the sky with nothing to worry about, which was always the whole point.
