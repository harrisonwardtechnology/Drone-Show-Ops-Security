# Contributing

Contributions welcome. **No One Is as Dumb as All of Us**, and this industry is young enough that every honest lesson written down makes the whole field safer.

## Useful

- Operator field notes, scrubbed to the lesson: what a site survey caught, what an abort taught, what a venue got wrong or wonderfully right.
- Venue-side experiences hiring shows, especially questions you wish you had asked.
- Near-miss patterns, anonymized hard. The lesson travels, the show name does not need to.
- Platform-agnostic practices. This repo names no manufacturers on purpose; patterns over products.

## Not Useful

- Anything attack-enabling. No interference techniques, no exploitation detail, no "how you would actually take over a show." Declined on principle, every time, per the [OT repo's identical line](https://github.com/HarrisonWard/OT-Security-for-IT-People).
- Anything show-identifiable or venue-identifiable without written permission.
- Counter-drone content. Different field, different lawyers.
- Vendor promotion. Tool and platform mentions are fine when they earn their place. Marketing isn't.

## How

Open an issue first for anything big. Small fixes straight to a PR.

## Style

Plain English, written for a crew chief and a venue manager at the same table. Say why, not just what. Short. The pre-show briefing is the format ideal.
